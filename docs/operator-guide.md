# Operator guide — KIR

Portal: e-tendering runs on both the Central Procurement Unit's own
official portal (`www.procurement.gov.ki`) and a third-party platform,
illion TenderLink (New Zealand). Entity: Ministry of Tourism, Commerce,
Industry and Cooperatives (MTCIC) business/company registration
(Companies Ordinance Cap10A) + Kiribati Tax Division TIN record
(requires a Business Certificate from the Ministry of Commerce and a
Business Operating Certificate from the Island Council). Procurement-
tier engagements (VLVP/LVP self-managed by the Procuring Entity vs.
MVP/HVP requiring Contract Award Committee / Central Contract Award
Board review, per the Public Procurement Manual 2020's own §7.1.6
four-tier AUD ladder) also clear the Manual's own accumulated-value
eligibility gate before a filing can proceed -- a recurring monthly-
rate engagement's value accumulates over its full duration, per the
Manual's own worked example. Foreign-investor engagements additionally
clear the Foreign Investment Guideline's own Schedule 1 Reserved-
activity gate. Human-gated filing only.
